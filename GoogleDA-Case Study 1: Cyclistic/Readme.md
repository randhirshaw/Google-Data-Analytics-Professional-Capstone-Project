# 🚴 Cyclistic Bike-Share Case Study  
## How Casual Riders and Annual Members Use Bikes Differently

### 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Cyclistic bike-share usage data to identify behavioral differences between casual riders and annual members. The goal is to generate data-driven insights that inform marketing strategies to convert casual riders into long-term members.

The analysis follows the **Google Data Analytics framework**:
Ask → Prepare → Process → Analyze → Share → Act

---

### 🎯 Business Question
How do casual riders and annual members use Cyclistic bikes differently, and how can these differences inform strategies to increase annual membership?

---

### 🧠 Key Insights

**Annual Members**
- Shorter, more frequent rides  
- Weekday commuting patterns  
- Consistent usage year-round  

**Casual Riders**
- Longer ride durations  
- Weekend and midday usage  
- Strong seasonality (spring & summer peaks)  

These patterns indicate commuter-oriented usage among members and leisure-oriented usage among casual riders.

### 📊 Tools & Techniques

### 🎯 Recommendations
- Target casual riders during weekends and peak leisure hours
- Promote annual membership as a cost-effective upgrade
- Emphasize reliability and everyday convenience in membership messaging
